Cultural Self-Assessment (Hofstede Model)

I am comfortable with clear hierarchy in the workplace.

I expect leaders to make final decisions without much consultation.

It is important to show respect to seniority, regardless of merit.

I feel uneasy challenging someone in authority.

Titles and formal positions matter a lot in how I treat others.

I prefer to work independently rather than in a group.

Personal achievement matters more to me than team success.

I feel comfortable making decisions that are good for me, even if others disagree.

Loyalty to my team or community is important to me.

I value standing out more than fitting in.

I admire people who are ambitious and assertive.

I believe success is measured primarily by achievements and results.

It’s important to compete and be the best.

I prefer cooperation over competition.

I value quality of life more than material success.

I feel uncomfortable with unstructured or ambiguous situations.

I prefer clear rules and policies at work.

I feel anxious if plans are not clearly defined.

I adapt quickly to change.

I like taking risks in new environments.

I believe persistence and long-term planning lead to success.

Traditions should guide decisions even if they delay progress.

I am willing to delay short-term rewards for future gain.

I find it hard to follow long-term plans.

I think flexibility is more important than sticking to old values.

I believe enjoying life and having fun is important.

I freely express my emotions and opinions.

I often feel guilty for indulging myself.

Life should be about enjoyment, not just duty.

I believe discipline is more important than pleasure.
